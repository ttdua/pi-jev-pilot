# Evidence: routing and thresholds, 2026-11-05

Measurements behind the design, from one operator's data. Aggregates only. Briefs, prompts and scripts are not in this repository.

**Read with care:** one person's work, a small sample, and no ground truth for quality. These results show where Jev routes work, never whether the routed model does it well.

## Data

| Source | Size |
|---|---|
| Decision ledger of the earlier port | 500 main turns over 5 days |
| Worker decisions in session files | 456 |
| Launches over 7 days | 532 |
| Replay sample | 46 worker briefs, stratified by agent, against `jev-1.14.0` |

**Fidelity.** Replaying with the port's payload matched 39 of 41 recorded effort levels, with a mean probability difference of .012. A rerun changed 2 of 46 effort answers and no tiers.

## Why worker routing was concentrated

Before any change: 450 Sonnet, 6 Opus, **0 Haiku**; effort `high` 63%, `medium` 20%, `xhigh` 10%, `low` 7%.

| Hypothesis | Test | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Haiku cannot run | Registry, past runs | Rejected |
| Silent fallback from `fast` | No `low` worker on the parent's Opus | Rejected |
| Policy inflates effort | Applied level against Jev's read | Rejected: always equal |
| Risk forces it | `risky` values | Rejected: never above .7 |
| Truncation at 2000 characters | Full brief | Rejected: 5 of 46 changed, in both directions |
| Anchoring on agent `thinking`, `model` | Removed them | Rejected: 4 of 46 changed |
| Agent names | Neutral name | Rejected: 4 of 46 changed |
| **Tier descriptions** | Rewrote them | **Confirmed** |

Tier was unchanged in 46 of 46 for all three rejected variants.

The workload matters too. Of 532 launches, `worker`-like agents took 215, review-like 189, workflows 120, and `scout` 7. The main session performed its own lookups (5801 `bash`, 390 `read`). Few bounded tasks reach a subagent at all.

## Routing by mode

Share is estimated from the launch mix per agent. One smoke-test brief is excluded, leaving 45.

| Rubric | Haiku | Sonnet | Opus | Fable | Reviewer slot | Estimated share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Upstream (no slot) | 1 | 45 | 0 | 0 | | |
| `budget` | 16 | 11 | 0 | 0 | 18 | Haiku 18, Sonnet 36, Sol 46 |
| `standard` | 6 | 19 | 2 | 0 | 18 | Haiku 4, Sonnet 43, Opus 7, Sol 46 |
| `premium` | 0 | 14 | 11 | 2 | 18 | Sonnet 14, Opus 33, Fable 7, Sol 46 |

Shifting thresholds on one rubric was also tried and rejected: Jev is often already sure of `balanced`, so workers barely moved, and low bars pushed scouting to Fable.

**Reviewer slot:** 18 of 18 review briefs chose it at probability ≥ .85, 0 of 28 others. With every agent renamed `general-purpose`: 18 of 18, 17 of them ≥ .6, 0 of 28 others. Other tiers were unchanged in 28 of 28.

**Model names in the rubric** changed 1 of 46, a near tie.

**Effort does not follow the rubric:** 44 of 46 unchanged in `budget`, 41 of 46 in `premium`. `high` is 57% of the worker sample.

**Expert:** `expert_needed` peaked at .20 under the original wording and .45 under the softer one, and `expert` was never the top choice. Hence the different rule in `premium`.

## Main session

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Asked, answered | 491 of 491 |
| Latency | Median 493 ms, p90 670 ms, max 1092 ms |
| Started at | `low` 58%, `high` 22%, `xhigh` 11%, `medium` 7% |
| Strategy advice attached | 5 of 500 |
| Turns marked corrected | 10 of 500 |

**Confidence does not predict large turns at low effort.** Share of `low` turns with at least 15 tool calls: 9% below .3, 6% at .3–.5, 2% at .5–.6, 9% at .6–.8, 12% at ≥ .8. Those 23 of 291 turns account for 29% of all output tokens. Tool count is only a rough proxy for size.

**Upstream's confidence bars would block most changes.** Applying .3, .5 and .6 to 221 effort changes blocks 118 (53%), in a simplified simulation.

**Upstream's tuning rules never fired**, whether cumulative or over the last 100 turns, checked every 20:

| Rule | Bar | Observed |
|---|---|---|
| Raise timeout | Over 10% unanswered | 0% |
| Raise lowering bar | Over 20% of low starts corrected | 2% |
| Raise bar for `high` | Over 60% easy | 8% |
| Lower margin | Over 60% of `xhigh` easy | 19% |

**Mid-turn raise would rarely fire.** Two or more consecutive tool failures: 2 of 500 turns.

## Cache and effort

Consecutive same-model requests under 4 minutes apart, over 200 sessions. A miss means cache reads below half of the previous prompt size.

| Provider, model | Requests after an effort change | Miss rate after a change | Miss rate without |
|---|---|---|---|
| `claude-code`, Opus 5.5 | 37 | 0% | 2% |
| `claude-code`, Sonnet 5.5 | 90 | 0% | 1% |
| `9router`, GPT-6.1 Sol | 190 | 58% | 2% |
| `9router`, GPT-6.1 Terra | 65 | 66% | 2% |

This is association over real use, not a controlled test.

## Threshold provenance

| Threshold | Origin | Trust |
|---|---|---|
| `fast` probability .6 | Chosen here to demote near ties | Low |
| Slot probability .6 | Upstream bar, moved to probability | Low; separated cleanly in replay |
| `risky > .7` | Upstream; wording tried on examples, the number was not | Low |
| Effort ceiling `xhigh` | Upstream replay of 76 prompts, 4 at `xhigh` | Low |
| `fast` effort cap `medium` | Chosen here | Low |
| `expert_needed > .9` | The port: "conservative, not calibrated" | Low; effectively never fires |
| Premium Fable rule | Chosen here; 2 cases | Low |
| Strategy .6 and .8 | Upstream; 12 requests tuned the wording, not the bar | Low; only advice |
| Skill .5 and .3 | Upstream; 16 prompts | Medium; not on this catalog |
| Tool removal .5 | The port; small benchmark, unreviewed labels | Low; hence `observe` |
| Tuning cooldown, revert window of 40 | Chosen here | Low |

All were measured, if at all, mostly in English. TypeSafe documents lower accuracy in other languages, and this operator writes in Vietnamese.
