# Evidence: routing by mode, 2026-11-07

This is the WG-12 replay. It measures the shipped mode rubrics and expert gates on real worker briefs. The figures are aggregates from one operator's data. Briefs, prompts and scripts are not in this repository.

**Read with care:** these results show where Jev routes work, not whether the routed model does it well.

## Data

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Source | Worker launches in session files, 2026-10-25 to 2026-11-07 |
| Briefs | 1101 unique (agent, brief) pairs. Median 1844 characters; 7% are longer than the 4000-character cap |
| Mix | 531 implementation launches, 508 reviews, 60 scout or verify, 2 advisory |
| Request | The package's own worker state and questions (`buildWorkerState`, `buildWorkerQuestions`) with all four tiers offered and registry display names. No slots, since WG-13 is not merged |
| Model | `jev-1.14.0` (`jev-latest`) |
| Variants | `budget`, `standard`, a plain rerun of `standard`, and `premium`. 4404 requests in total, with no misses |
| Latency | Median 220 ms, p90 289 ms, max 790 ms |

## Routing by mode

This is the final tier after the decision order (`decideTier`), as a share of launches.

| Mode | `fast` | `balanced` | `deep` | `expert` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `budget` | 73% | 22% | 5% | 0% |
| `standard` | 3% | 63% | 34% | 0% |
| `standard` rerun | 3% | 62% | 34% | 0% |
| `premium` | 0% | 44% | 47% | 9% |

**Noise:** the plain rerun changed the tier of 25 of 1101 briefs (2%) and the effort of 41.

By launch kind:

| Mode | Kind | n | `fast` | `balanced` | `deep` | `expert` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `budget` | implementation | 531 | 62% | 34% | 4% | 0% |
| `budget` | review | 508 | 86% | 9% | 5% | 0% |
| `standard` | implementation | 531 | 6% | 75% | 19% | 0% |
| `standard` | review | 508 | 0% | 55% | 45% | 0% |
| `standard` | scout or verify | 60 | 3% | 25% | 72% | 0% |
| `premium` | implementation | 531 | 0% | 47% | 44% | 9% |
| `premium` | review | 508 | 0% | 42% | 51% | 7% |

**Moves from `standard`:**
- `budget` moved 890 of 1101 launches to a cheaper tier: 571 from `balanced` to `fast`, 195 from `deep` to `fast` and 124 from `deep` to `balanced`.
- `premium` moved 418 launches. 262 went from `balanced` to `deep` and 98 to `expert`; 32 went from `fast` to `balanced` and 25 from `deep` to `balanced`.

**Expert:**
- In `budget` and `standard`, `expert_needed` peaked at .55, so the gate never passed.
- In `premium`, the gate admitted 98 launches (9%) and denied 40 that chose `expert` with too little effort. Under the softer wording, `expert_needed` reached .95.

**Effort does not follow the mode:** `high` is 71% of the worker decisions in every variant.

## Against the 2026-11-05 replay

The [earlier replay](2026-11-05-replay.md#routing-by-mode) used 45 briefs, a reviewer slot and the earlier port's payload. Its non-review launches (n = 27) are compared here with today's non-review launches (n = 593).

| Mode | 2026-11-05 `fast` / `balanced` / `deep` / `expert` | 2026-11-07 | Within the earlier sample's 95% interval |
|---|---|---|---|
| `budget` | 59 / 41 / 0 / 0 | 62 / 33 / 5 / 0 | Yes |
| `premium` | 0 / 52 / 41 / 7 | 0 / 45 / 44 / 11 | Yes |
| `standard` | 22 / 70 / 7 / 0 | 6 / 69 / 25 / 0 | No: less `fast`, more `deep` |

`budget` and `premium` reproduce. `standard` does not, and the difference is larger than the noise.

Two causes are possible, and neither is tested:
- The workload changed. Half of today's non-review launches are `superspec.*` implementers with long, fully specified briefs. `scout` chooses `deep` 72% of the time.
- The request changed. The earlier replay used the earlier port's payload. This one uses the package's state with model names in the criteria.

The modes shift work in the intended directions. The operator's target mix per mode, and the rubric work it needs, are tracked in [#39](https://github.com/ttdua/pi-jev-pilot/issues/39).
