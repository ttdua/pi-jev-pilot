# Evidence: tier mix by mode, 2026-11-07

This is the done-when replay for [#39](https://github.com/ttdua/pi-jev-pilot/issues/39). It routes the [WG-12 replay](2026-11-07-replay.md) briefs through the code of [ADR 0008](../decisions/0008-mode-target-cutpoints.md): one worker rubric, the difficulty score and cut points from a rolling window. The figures are aggregates from one operator's data. Briefs, answers and scripts are not in this repository.

**Read with care:** these results measure where Jev routes work, not whether the routed model does it well. One operator's workload over two weeks is a small sample. The non-review figures differ by scenario, as described under [Mix by mode](#mix-by-mode).

## Mix by mode

These are final tier shares after `decideTier`, as a share of launches, rounded to whole percents. The in-range verdicts use unrounded shares. A share is **in range** within ±10 points of its target. For `expert`, in range means at most the cap plus 10 points. Every launch was routed: no launch fell back to "none".

Three ways of reading the 593 non-review launches:
- **All:** every launch is routed, and all launches feed the window.
- **Non-review in the mixed stream:** the non-review launches of the same routing. This is the case of a configured reviewer slot that the model does not choose, so reviews stay in the window.
- **Non-review stream:** only non-review launches are routed and fill the window. This is the case where a reviewer slot takes every review, since slot launches never enter the window.

## Reading

- **All launches:** every mode is in range in the run and the rerun. Over 400-launch windows, `budget` and `premium` stay in range, and `standard` misses 2 of 15 by up to 12.8 points.
- **Non-review stream:** every mode is in range. This is the case where a reviewer slot takes the reviews.
- **Non-review in the mixed stream:** every mode is out of range, with a share 10.2–12.6 points from its target. In this workload, reviews score higher than implementation briefs here, so cut points fitted on the mixed window send more non-review launches to a cheaper tier. The cut points hold the target for the launches that fill the window, not for one kind of launch within it.

No values were tuned after this replay. The seed and targets are the ones shipped.
